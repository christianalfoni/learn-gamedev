# Untitled Game

> This is the source of truth. Everything on the site is derived from it, and new
> assignments come from what you add here. Write in whatever detail you have —
> a single honest sentence beats a paragraph of placeholder.
>
> **How the structure works:** `##` headings are areas, `###` headings are features.
> Every `###` needs a `{#id}` so assignments can point at it. Delete anything that
> doesn't apply to your game, and add whatever does.

## The game

### Pitch {#pitch}

*One or two sentences. What the player does, and what makes it worth doing.*

### Pillars {#pillars}

*Three things this game is, that you'd refuse to compromise. These decide arguments later.*

## Mechanics

### Movement {#movement}

*How the player moves. Ground feel, air control, anything beyond walking.*

### Core verb {#core-verb}

*The one thing the player does over and over. Attacking, grappling, building, talking.*

## Art

### Player character {#player-art}

*Resolution, palette size, silhouette, what animations exist.*

### Environment {#environment-art}

*Tilesets, materials, how many, what mood.*

## World

### Level structure {#levels}

*How levels are built and connected. Size, layers, what a level contains.*

## Audio

### Sound effects {#sfx}

*What makes noise, and what it should feel like.*

### Music {#music}

*How many tracks, when they change, whether they layer.*

## Feel

### Game feel {#game-feel}

*Screenshake, hitstop, particles — the feedback that isn't mechanics.*
